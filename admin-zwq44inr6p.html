Backtest one stock — which strategy worked best?

Pick a stock. The app replays its price history and tests 19 strategies on it — the scanner’s 12 chart-pattern signals, the scanner’s own shortlist and 6 classic trading rules — then compares them with simply buying and holding.

Backtest the whole scanner (all stocks)

Replays the scan on past sessions (every 5 trading days), takes the top picks from each scan, and simulates the trade plan: buy-stop fill at the entry zone, exit at target, stop, or after the holding period. Also shows how well the probability estimates matched what actually happened.

◆ Strategy watch

Pair a stock with a strategy — usually the one that worked best for it in the backtest. After every scan the app checks whether that strategy gives a buy or sell signal today and alerts you. “Auto” always follows whichever strategy currently has the best backtest.
